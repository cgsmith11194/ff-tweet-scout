It's a model that predicts how many fantasy points an "average" player would score on each specific play. A carry at the 2-yard line with 8:00 minutes remaining in the 3rd quarter and the score tied is worth more than a 1st-and-10 carry at the 45-yard line with 1:30 left in the 4th quarter with a 24-10 lead.
